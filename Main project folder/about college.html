About College

  • The College of Science and Technology (CST) operates a competitive selection process. With an expanding roster of professional engineering tracks, it continues to serve as Bhutan's specialized hub for technical training.

    • Undergraduate Admission Requirements

    To secure admission to a CST undergraduate degree, applicants must meet strict criteria set by the Royal University of Bhutan:

  • Academic Requirements: A pass in the Bhutan Higher Secondary Education Certificate (BHSEC / Class XII) or equivalent, with a minimum of 50% each in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English, plus a pass mark in Dzongkha.

  • Entrance/Aptitude Exams: Applicants must pass an online entrance test or an architecture aptitude test administered by the college, scoring at least 50% to qualify for selection.

  • Identity: A valid Bhutanese Citizenship Identity Card (CID) is mandatory for domestic intake registration.
