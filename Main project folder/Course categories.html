Course Categories Description
Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering is the study of designing, constructing, and maintaining buildings, roads, bridges, dams, and other infrastructure. It applies science and mathematics to create safe, strong, and sustainable structures.
Electrical Engineering

Deals with electricity, electrical systems, power generation, transmission, and electrical equipment.
Electronics & Communication Engineering

Focuses on electronic devices, communication systems, signals, networks, and modern communication technologies.
Information Technology (IT)

Covers computer systems, software, databases, networks, web development, and information management.
Architecture

Focuses on designing buildings and spaces that are functional, safe, attractive, and environmentally suitable.
Engineering Geology

Studies rocks, soil, and geological conditions to support the safe construction of buildings, roads, tunnels, and other structures.
Instrumentation & Control Engineering

Deals with sensors, measurement systems, automation, and control of industrial processes and machines.
Water Resources Engineering

Focuses on the management, development, and conservation of water resources, including rivers, dams, and water supply systems.
Mechanical Engineering

Deals with the design, manufacturing, operation, and maintenance of machines, engines, and mechanical systems.
Software Engineering

Focuses on designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software applications and computer systems.
